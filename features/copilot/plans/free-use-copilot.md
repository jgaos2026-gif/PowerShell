# Free Use Copilot

This document defines a starter policy for using Copilot in low-risk, public, and approved workflows.

## Allowed uses

- Drafting documentation and repository notes
- Creating starter code, scripts, and test templates
- Brainstorming workflow structure and operational plans
- Producing summaries, checklists, and implementation outlines

## Guardrails

- Do not expose secrets, credentials, tokens, or private customer data.
- Do not change production systems, billing flows, or access controls without explicit approval.
- Do not bypass repository rules, CI checks, or branch protections.
- Review generated content before publishing or merging.

## Escalation rules

Escalate immediately when a task involves:

- financial transactions or billing
- legal, compliance, or policy interpretation
- access to restricted systems
- sensitive personal or customer data

## Operating posture

Use Copilot as a supportive assistant for planning and drafting, while keeping human review and auditability at the center of execution.
